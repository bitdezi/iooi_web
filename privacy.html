Privacy Policy

iooi | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

iooi is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users.

What We Don't Collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Financial data or cryptocurrency wallet information
  • Search history or usage patterns
  • Device identifiers or tracking data
  • Analytics or behavioral data

2. How the App Works

iooi operates entirely on your local device:

  • All searches are processed locally on your Mac
  • Search history is stored only on your device
  • No data is transmitted to our servers
  • External API calls are made directly from your device to cryptocurrency data providers

3. Third-Party Services

iooi connects to external cryptocurrency data APIs and trading platforms. When you use our app:

  • API calls to cryptocurrency data providers are subject to their privacy policies
  • Clicking external links (trading platforms, block explorers) will take you to third-party websites
  • We do not control or monitor these external interactions

4. Data Storage

All user data remains on your device:

  • Search history is cached locally using LRU algorithm (maximum 20 recent searches)
  • User preferences and settings are stored in local application files
  • No cloud storage or remote backup of user data

5. Data Security

Since we don't collect user data, there's no risk of data breaches from our side. However:

  • Your device's security measures protect locally stored data
  • External API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • We recommend keeping your macOS updated for optimal security

6. Children's Privacy

iooi is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us:
